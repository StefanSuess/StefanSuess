### Bobert

[Bobert](https://github.com/StefanSuess/Bobert) is a free extension for the Kiro IDE that keeps track of your credits. It shows your budget in the status bar, forecasts whether your credits will last until the reset, estimates what extra credits would cost and points out files Kiro doesn't need to read. It runs entirely on your machine.
